Lucky Quest

Roll the die and walk the 20-space path to the finish. Pick up treasure on the way and watch out for sinkholes that drag you backward. Play solo or pass the screen around with up to four players.

How many players?

Players take turns on this screen. Treasure goes to whoever lands on it first. The game ends when someone reaches the finish, and the highest score wins.
